# Geo-map-camera-pro
Geo Map Camera Pro: capture photos with live GPS location, map preview, date/time and address stamped on every shot. Open-source geotagging camera app. Add location, coordinates, map snapshot and timestamp overlays to your photos. A free, local-first GPS camera: stamp photos with map, coordinates, address and weather. No account, no upload.
Geo Map Camera Pro lets you take photos with location details stamped right on the image: GPS coordinates, full address, a mini map, date and time, and optional notes. Useful for site inspections, field work, travel logs, deliveries and proof-of-location photos.
Features
Live map and GPS stamp on every photo
Customizable overlay (template, font, position)
Date, time, address and compass data
Works offline, with photos saved to your device
Easy sharing and export
